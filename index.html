Every germ leaves a drop

Each germ you beat leaves a drop of plasma that drifts down the vessel. Drops that touch merge into a bigger drop worth more. Any drop that drifts past the bottom is lost.

So every second asks two things: what to spend, and when to swipe. Sweep early and keep it safe, or let the drops gather for a bigger catch.

Small cells, big tide

Let the drops grow

Drops merge as they drift, +1 for every merge. Catch a whole line in one stroke, but what slips past the bottom is gone for good.

Ten immune cells

Start with Neutrophils and B-cells. Each first win lets you pick a new cell or an upgrade. Macrophages swallow small germs, Platelets plug the vessel, Killer T cells take on the big ones.

60 vessels, nothing locked

Six body regions with new germs as you go deeper. Every vessel can be played without buying anything; boosters are optional.

From fingertip to heart

Ten vessels per region, each one or two minutes long. Win with all five heart marks left for three stars. Lose, and you are back in the vessel at once with your cells kept.
