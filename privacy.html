Privacy Policy

Plasmatide · effective October 30, 2026

Plasmatide is a game for iPhone. It does not ask who you are, and it does not collect, share or sell any data about you.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: which vessels are open, your best stars for each vessel, the immune cells you have collected and upgraded, and your boosters (Fever Spike, Antibody Flood, Second Wind).
  • Your settings: the five cells you take into a vessel and whether the Lab Stains look is switched on.
  • Whether you have bought Vial Pack, Lab Cooler or Lab Stains, and the store transaction IDs already credited, so the same purchase is never added twice.

This is stored on your device only and never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Purchases

All 60 vessels are free to play. The optional purchases (Vial Pack, Lab Cooler and Lab Stains) are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store under Apple's own privacy policy. We receive no payment details or personal information; the game only learns from the App Store which purchases exist.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
