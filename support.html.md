# Plasmatide Support

Sweep the plasma. Hold the heart.

## Contact

Write to [lam@textwiz.pro](mailto:lam@textwiz.pro). Please say which iPhone you play on and, if it is about a vessel, its number.

## How to play

- **Swipe the plasma:** every germ you beat leaves a mint drop that drifts down the vessel. Drag your finger across the drops to collect them. Drops that touch merge into a bigger one (+1 for each merge); drops that drift past the bottom are lost.
- **Call cells:** tap a round card at the bottom when you have enough plasma. The cell hatches at the heart and swims down to meet the germs.
- **Hold the heart:** each germ that reaches the heart costs heart marks. Lose all five and the vessel is overrun; clear every wave to win. Finish with all five marks for three stars.
- **New cells:** the first time you clear a vessel, pick a new cell or an upgrade. Choose up to five cells to take in from *Cells* on the map; *Suggested* picks a set for the next region.
- **Boosters** (from vessel 6): Fever Spike hits every germ in the vessel, Antibody Flood slows and marks them for 10 seconds, and Second Wind brings back three heart marks once per run when you are overrun.

## Purchases

All 60 vessels are free to play. *Vial Pack* (4 Fever Spike, 4 Antibody Flood, 2 Second Wind) and *Lab Cooler* (12, 12 and 6) add boosters. *Lab Stains* is a one-time purchase that recolours the vessel, cells and plasma in purple and pink; the rules stay the same. Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Bought Lab Stains before, or on another device with the same Apple Account? Open the settings button on the map and tap *Restore Purchases*, or use *Restore Purchases* in the Shop. Boosters are used up as you play, so they cannot be restored after the app is deleted.

## Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
